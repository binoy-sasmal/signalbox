### what this is ###
The memory sizing term PLAN.md section 7 names:
  single-message decode peak x queue depth x safety factor.

It is measured here rather than taken from Stage 0 because the Stage 0
field does not hold that quantity -- it reports 2.14 GB for VBB, whose
bodies decompress to 9 MB. See runs/stage0-corrections/.

The instrument is RSS, not tracemalloc, and the tracemalloc column is
carried alongside to keep the reason visible: protobuf's upb runtime
allocates its arena in C++, so tracemalloc reports ~240 bytes for
decoding megabytes. Both numbers, same decode, same process.

Inputs are three real Stage 0 payloads, one per feed. They are under
runs/gate5/blobs/ and gitignored, like every other captured payload.
